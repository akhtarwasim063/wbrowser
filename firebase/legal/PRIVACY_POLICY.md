# WBrowser: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

WBrowser is an Android web browser. This policy explains what the app shares with the service administrator and how
it is stored.

## What the WBrowser app sends
- The name you enter on first launch and a randomly generated user ID.
- The websites and page addresses (URLs) you visit, visit times, time spent, and session length.
- Your phone model, Android version and app version.
- Optionally, your Google name and email if you sign in with Google, and your search queries only if you switch that on.

We do **not** collect passwords, cookies, login tokens, page contents, form data, or anything from private tabs.
Sensitive parts of web addresses (tokens, codes, keys) are removed on your phone before sending.

## How the data is used
Only the administrators of this service can view it, to understand browser usage. It is not sold or shared with third
parties.

## Where it is stored
Data is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) and protected by Firebase Authentication and security rules: each
user can only write their own data, and only signed-in administrators can read it. Google processes this data as our
cloud provider. Signing in with Google uses Google's official sign-in and gives us only your name and email; we never
see your Google password.

## Your choices
In the app, open Settings > Account and privacy to pause sharing, turn off search sharing, unlink Google, or delete all
your data. You can also ask the administrator to delete your data.
